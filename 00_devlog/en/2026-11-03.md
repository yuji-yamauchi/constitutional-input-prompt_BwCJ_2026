# 2026-11-03 — The day the design was written, and walked back three steps

> **In short.** Day 2. The requirements document went from v0.1 to v0.3 — not
> because it grew, but because two things in it turned out to be wrong. A claim
> about medical-device rules had been carried from a tagged guess into a stated
> fact, and it took three steps to walk back. The design itself did not change.
>
> The Japanese original is at [`../ja/2026-11-03.md`](../ja/2026-11-03.md).

## Written

- **The requirements document** — what it is for, its three functions, **what it was
  decided not to do**, and 25 acceptance criteria in a checkable form.
- **The screen specification** — screens, components, wording, colour, and the rules
  for small screens.
- **The specification for the overall diagram** — instructions for making a clean
  copy of a hand-drawn figure without changing its structure.

## Got wrong (1) — a tag was promoted

About a particular restriction, one writer wrote it with the tag **[inferred]**.

The next writer quoted it as **[secondary confirmation]**.

The one after that **widened the scope**, writing that it appeared "nowhere in the
record".

Reading the primary text directly showed that **the first tag had been right.**

It could be walked back **because the first tag had not been deleted.** Three steps
back, and each step was rewritten to say what it had actually rested on.

**Kept as a type:** when you quote, copy **the tag along with the text**, not the text
alone. When you write "there is none", write **where you looked, on the same line**.
What you can write is only "none in the range I searched".

## Got wrong (2) — a clock was guessed at

Timestamps written into several documents **had been written without reading a
clock**. They were two to three hours early. The order of events did not change, but
as a record it was wrong.

**Kept as a type:** read the clock once before writing a time.

## Decided

What the measurement points at was changed from **"improvement"** to **"change"**.

The reason is on one line in the record: *"I want them to have the perception that
at the very least, they themselves can change."*

The difference is not small. "Improvement" means better or worse along a scale.
"Change" means only **not fixed**. And the tool does not **return a judgment** saying
"you have changed". The one who looks is the person.

Words that will not appear on screen were fixed: points, levels, rank, comparison
with last time, progress, growth, improvement.
