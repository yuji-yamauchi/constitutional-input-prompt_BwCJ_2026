## Decided

- **The submission repository was created** (after the kickoff). Contents: a README
  and empty folders, nothing else.
- **The rules and the official Q&A were gathered onto one sheet**, in a form that
  keeps *settled*, *inferred* and *unverified* **separate**. What the rules do not
  say is kept as "not yet asked", not as "allowed".
- **The licensing shape was decided**: source code open; images and names not reusable.

## Got wrong

**A figure another reader said they had measured was treated as settled without
being checked here.**

It was a sentence about the scope of a credit. The original measurement was wrong,
so the document written on top of it was wrong too. It was corrected the next day
against the primary source — the grant notice itself.

**Kept as a type:** do not promote someone else's "verified" one step while it is
still unread.
