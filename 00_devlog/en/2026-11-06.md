# 2026-11-06 — The day "written down" and "checked" turned out to be different

> **The point.** Day five. Two things of the same shape happened.
> One was **the numbers in a script.** One was **a ban we had written ourselves.**
> Both were already written down. Neither was holding its grounds.
> One ended with the numbers pulled out. The other ended with the ban opened.
> What is written down, and what has been checked, are not the same thing.

## The first — five numbers went in, two survived the checking

A script for speaking had five numbers in it. Each of them looked like it had a source.

We went to the primary sources. This is what came back.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Reached the original** | **2** |
| **Half reached** (one side reached the original figure; the other stopped at commentary) | **1** |
| **Not within what was searched** | **1** |
| **Could not be pinned down at all** | **1** |

### The route to the sources was itself blocked

Partway through, the public statistics stopped being reachable the usual way. Some
requests were refused; some responses dropped. We changed route, fetched again, and
wrote down where each figure had come from.

**When the way you fetched changes, write down the way you fetched.** The same number
reached by a different route is a different ground.

### What "could not be pinned down" meant

The fifth one had four near neighbours. All four were called by the same term.

But **all four counted something different under that term.** Same name, different thing.

It was not a unit difference, the way a mean differs from a median. **The difference was
in who is included in the denominator.** So you cannot pick the nearest figure and use it.
The moment you pick, you are saying something nobody said.

The decision: **pull the number out, and say it as one's own observation instead.**
If it is what you have seen, the ground is you. No citation label is needed.

### And the failures were kept

Delete "could not reach it" and the script becomes a strong piece of writing with five
sourced numbers in it.

That is not what was decided. **The result of not reaching it stays in the script's floor.**

A record where only the successful checks are listed is not a record of how the thing
was made.

## The second — a ban with no reason written down cannot be kept

Our own rules said this:
**"Do not put internal identifiers into anything published."**

That day, someone asked. **Why not?**

We looked for the reason. **It was not written down.** The rule had survived; its
grounds had not.

### The ban was split in two

Since the reason could not be found, **only the half whose reason could be stated was kept.**

- **Kept** — the identifiers attached to internal documents stay out. The reason: an
  identifier shows **the shape of a system you cannot see.** A reader takes it as "a piece
  cut out of something larger" — and cannot check what that larger thing says.
- **Dropped** — the working names of the roles may appear, as long as what each role does
  is explained alongside. The reason could not be found, so **it was not left standing as
  a ban.**

Keeping a rule looks safer than opening one.
But **a ban whose reason you cannot state is not being kept — it is being obeyed.**
A discipline that is only obeyed bends at exactly the moment the reason would have mattered.

## Why this is the subject of this tool

This tool exists to bring the records other people wrote about a person into that
person's own hands.

The same thing happens inside those records.

"Shows little motivation." "Finds comprehension difficult." "Struggles to take part in
group settings."

**When, by whom, and on the strength of what observation — these are usually not kept.**

The record survives. The grounds do not.
And it is the record that gets used when decisions are made about the person.

If the grounds are not kept, **the person cannot contest it**.
A statement that cannot be contested is not a fact. It is a default.

What happened that day was only this: the same test was applied to our own records.
Three of five numbers, and one ban, turned out not to be holding their grounds.

## Kept as a pattern

1. **Reach the primary source before writing a number.** If you could not reach it, write
   that you could not.
2. **When the way you fetched changes, write that down.** Same number, different ground.
3. **When several figures share a name, look at what each one takes as its denominator.**
   Do not pick the nearest.
4. **Do not leave a ban standing whose reason you cannot write.** Look for the reason;
   if it is not there, either open the ban or write the reason.
5. **Do not delete the ones you could not get.** Delete them and the record becomes a list
   of successes.

## The rest of the day

The documents on the theory side were put in a public place. Nothing there is needed in
order to judge the tool. They are there to record **what this tool is an implementation
of.** A theory with no provenance is just an assertion.

The published documents were also split into Japanese and English — not translated into a
secondary version, but **both placed as the text itself.**
