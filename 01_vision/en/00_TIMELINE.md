# How this got here — a timeline of the theory

> **How this got here.** A dated trail of the theory behind this tool, from the
> first policy draft to the ideals document the submitted app is built on. Dates
> come from the filenames of the originals and from passages inside the documents
> published in this folder; where a document only refers to something not
> published here, that is marked. Nothing in this list is reconstructed from
> memory.
>
> The Japanese original is at [`../ja/00_TIMELINE.md`](../ja/00_TIMELINE.md).

This tool (Constitutional Input Prompt) was not thought up in the six days of the
hackathon. What was stacked up before it is set out here by date.

**How to read the marks**
`[shelf]` = the document itself, placed in [`../rinen/`](../rinen/) ／
`[text]` = something written in the body of a document placed here ／
`[outside]` = an original in the non-public workspace (not readable here)

## 2026-07-14 — the hierarchy had been standing on its head

> **The core theory (deductive self / inductive self / colonisation / adaptive
> preference / the three factors) appeared 0 times in the topmost theory
> document, and was written only on the app-layer policy side.**

was built as the correction.

## What is not in this timeline

- **The personal course of events at the origin.** That practice came before theory
  is in the record, but it touches individuals and family, so it is not written
  here.
- **The full diff of what changed in each version.** Versions are kept without
  overwriting, but what is published is the current version.
- **Undated ideas.** Anything that came up in conversation and was never left with
  a date in any document cannot be traced.
