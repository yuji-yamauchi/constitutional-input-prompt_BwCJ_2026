## They are published as they are

These are **internal working documents that have not been cleaned up.** They have
not been rewritten to read well.

- **Version histories, source tags, marks for "not yet verified", and "waiting on
  whose reply"** are all still in them. That itself is the record of how this was
  arrived at.
- **The folder and file names point at places that are not public.** They cannot be
  followed.
- `★` is an emphasis mark used internally.
- `[一次確認]` / `[2次一致]` / `[未確認]` are **tags for the strength of the grounds**:
  *primary source reached* / *agreement between secondary sources* / *unverified*.
  Only something whose original text the writer reached themselves is
  `[一次確認]`.

They should be hard to read. **Making them read well would drop the tags and the
versions.** Not dropping them was the choice made.
