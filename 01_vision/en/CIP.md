# CIP — Constitutional Input Prompt

> **Two of two.** This page and [SoE.md](SoE.md) are self-contained: either can be
> read alone. SoE is the frame — what a floor is and why good intentions cannot be
> the guarantee. CIP is the mechanism: the right to speak as the author of
> yourself, **before** a record about you is written. Both are edited from the
> working documents in [`../rinen/`](../rinen/), which remain the originals.
>
> The Japanese original is at [`../ja/CIP.md`](../ja/CIP.md). Where the two

## 1. The subject is not the AI

**The subject of CIP (Constitutional Input Prompt) is the person.**

Its former name was Input Constitutional AI (ICAI), and its subject was **the AI**
("give the AI a constitution, and have it protect rights at the input stage").
In the 2026 redefinition, **the subject was turned over to the person.**

> **Making it a basic human right to speak as a deductive self, *before* receiving
> inductive labelling.**

**That is: CIP means returning the authorship of the inductive self to the
person.**

*(Inductive self = the picture of you that others observe and infer. See
[SoE.md §3](SoE.md).)*

### The one-sentence definition

> **CIP is the principle that, at the stage where a support record is created, a
> person's will and preferences are received as "input" without passing through
> summary, translation, selection or eligibility screening, and are placed under
> that person's control.**

There is a range **this one sentence does not yet cover** (§5, §9). What it covers
is only the middle one of the three layers.

---

## 2. The most common error

**Do not write "enabling people to write high-quality prompts".**

The correct formulation is **"a prompt as one's own will"**.

**That is: do not re-read agency as skill.** The moment it is re-read, CIP
degenerates into **a training programme.**

A training programme creates better and worse. Better and worse create screening.
Screening creates **a will that failed the screening.** That is the same shape as
the thing CIP is trying to break.

---

## 3. Why the input stage

**Filtering at the output stage cannot compensate for structural exclusion at the
input stage.**

Unless the voices of disabled people enter the record — which eventually becomes
training data for support algorithms — **in their own words**, the system
**reproduces and amplifies** that exclusion in its output.

Hence the need to establish **"the moment at which a record is created"** as a
site of rights protection in its own right.

Fixing the output is too late. The reader has already finished filling in.

---

## 4. The four walls where input breaks

| | Wall | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **The wall of the form** | The individual support plan has no field for the "life level" (family, relationships). **You cannot order what is not on the menu.** |
| **2** | **The wall of translation** | Said aloud, it is converted into the support side's grammar — "let's start with employment" — and **the original words do not survive into the record.** |
| **3** | **The wall of disqualification (internalised)** | "I have no right to want anything" is internalised, and the wish **disappears before it becomes language.** The prompt is never formed. |
| **4** | **The wall of the receiving OS** | Asserted in the language of rights, **the receiver is not running the human-rights OS.** The gap between the promise (de jure) and the practice (de facto). |

The four are separate walls. **Break one, and if the other three stand, the voice
does not arrive.**

---

## 5. CIP's three layers

| Layer | Content | Walls broken |
|---|---|---|
| **Eligibility** | **Restoring the right to want.** Breaks the given that "receiving a benefit = drawing down an advance on your right to wish". | 3 |
| **Reception** | **Reception without an audition.** The words of a *person*, still holding their goodness, badness and foolishness, are received as input without casting screening. | 1, 2, 4 |
| **Means** | **Guaranteeing the communication standard for conveying thought** (putting into language, arithmetic, number systems). The **unperformed educational guarantee, performed late** — logically entailed by Article 1 of the Basic Act on Education, "the full development of personality". | 2, 3 |

**CIP is a shield and a forge at the same time.**

Protecting alone is not enough, because the words to be protected have sometimes
not yet taken shape.

---

## 6. Where the LLM stands

**It is a prosthesis of translation, until the third layer (means) is rebuilt.**

It stands between the person's expression and society's protocol.

**But it does not ghost-write the person's voice.**
**Automatic personality inference by an LLM is wholly prohibited** (canon R49;
grounds: low validity, and the amplification of discrimination by disability,
nationality and religion through mimicry prompts).
**This is blocked structurally, not by an operating rule.**

A prosthesis does not walk in place of the person walking.

---

## 7. Record sovereignty has a limit

**Record sovereignty is not realised without literacy.**

Saying "your record is yours" means nothing if the person **cannot read or
write**; the sovereignty ends as words on paper.

**So: support with language is a precondition of CIP, not an added feature.**

But — the prohibition in [SoE.md §6-2](SoE.md) bears here too.
**The moment it is explained as "a device for cultivating the deductive self", it
inverts into a device of enlightenment.**
**Language is a condition, not a goal.**

---

## 8. It includes the right not to keep

The person's control is not only **the right to keep.**

- **The freedom not to keep** comes first.
- **The way in to keeping** is the person's consent. The default is "do not keep".
- **What is kept** is the person's paint. It is not first of all someone else's
  assessment material.

Control over a record is **both the right to erase and the right to accumulate.**
It extends to **the person being able to choose what to store up** as material for
a time when they show their own course in their own words — putting together
documents for a job application, for instance.

And — **interpretation is not stored.** What is kept is only verbatim fact (the
time, and the person's choices and words).
**The device never writes a derived score or a trend classification.**

**Drawing a self-portrait happens when the person picks up the paint.**

---

## 9. The armour for taking this outside

When this principle is taken outside — to a conference, an institution, another
provider — **two things must always go with it.**

1. **Do not over-promise.** CIP is a principle that structurally holds down **one**
   of the four walls. It is not a device that breaks all four.
2. **You are also a subject of it.** A provider that flies the CIP flag is **also one
   of the actors holding the incentive that "it is lower-burden if the person does
   not decide"** ([SoE.md §9](SoE.md)). **No exceptions.**

---

## 10. What is not settled yet

This principle is **published unfinished.**

- **The range of the one-sentence definition.** The qualifier "at the stage where a
  support record is created" **covers only the reception layer** of the three.
  Eligibility and means do not fit inside the stage of creating a record. The means
  layer includes collecting on the unperformed delivery of compulsory education.
- **Effectiveness and the burden of proof.** A right "existing" and a right "biting"
  are different things. That text is undrafted.
- **The derivation from Article 1 of the Basic Act on Education** (the domestic-law
  anchor for the third layer) has not yet been merged into the canon.

**None of this is hidden.** The original,
`../rinen/2026_10_12_cip_rinensho_draft_v0_1.md`, still carries the list of open
questions as it stands.

---

## 11. The relation to SoE

- **SoE** fixes where the floor is, and who repays what.
- **CIP** fixes **at which stage** that floor is made to bite — **at the moment the
  record is created.**

SoE alone ends as a fine ideal.
CIP alone does not say what the protection is for.

**The two together are one body of theory.**

---

## 12. The implementation of this principle

The tool in this repository is CIP's first implementation.

- **Collect the records about yourself into your own hands** (the original is not
  altered; a copy is made).
- **When handing one outward, send the copy rather than the original, and keep a
  stub.**
- **Do not judge, by default** (it returns a question, and reads the person's own
  text back to them; it does not rewrite, summarise or score).

How to run it is in the repository [README](../../README.md). The record of the six
days in which it was made is in [`../../00_devlog/en/`](../../00_devlog/en/).
