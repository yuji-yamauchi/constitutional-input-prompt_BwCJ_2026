# 08_sources — Where the checking is written down

This tool says it puts **statutory text on screen exactly as the original reads.**
Anyone can say that, so **the checking is written down here.**

The Japanese original is at [`../ja/`](../ja/).

---

## What goes here

- **Copies of statutes, and the report of comparing them against the original**
- Research reports: what was looked up, where it was reached, what was found, and
  what could not be found

## How they are written

1. **Say where you reached.** Not "we looked into it", but which source, which
   version, reached how.
2. **Separate three strengths of grounds.**
   - **Primary** — reached the original text yourself and copied it verbatim
   - **Secondary agreement** — commentaries or reporting agree, but the original
     was not reached
   - **Unverified** — not reached
3. **Do not write "there is none."** What can be written is "**not within what was
   searched**" — with where you searched, on the same line.
4. **When a number is given, how it was counted goes on the same line.** A number
   that cannot be accounted for is not given.
5. **Do not delete what could not be established.** Delete it and the report
   becomes a list of successes.
