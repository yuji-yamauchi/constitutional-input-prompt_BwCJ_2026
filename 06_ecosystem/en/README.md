# 06_ecosystem — Who else is around it

What this tool reaches is the person, and whoever the person decides to hand
something to. What is needed beyond that, and who would carry it — **not built in
this submission; the thinking is kept here.**

The Japanese original is at [`../ja/`](../ja/).

---

## 1. What this tool can promise

The limits first.

| Layer | Whose work | What it can do |
|---|---|---|
| **One person at a time** | The support office and its staff — our work | **Make it visible. That is all** |
| **Social security (the system)** | Law and institutions, as agreed | **Absorb it — the system carries the cost** |

**An office can only make things visible. Recording a debt is not repaying it.**

Writing something into the ledger does not lighten anyone's load. What actually
carries the load is the institutional layer, and that layer is politics.

So a single office does not say "this solves it".

---

## 2. Read it as public health

Behind this tool are **three independent ways of reading** how a society's
foundations give way.

| | What it names |
|---|---|
| **Anomie** | Norms loosening, the fraying of social ties. **Having nothing left to lose destroys a society's predictability** |
| **Hubris** | Excess and arrogance on the side that holds power. **Power exempting itself from being checked** |
| **Hypnos** | Resignation, silence, numbed perception. **Receiving the definition of oneself from others, continuously** |

### Discipline in reading them

- **The three are independent.** They are not paired off against each other.
- **They are not sequenced.** Not "anomie, then hubris, then hypnos".
- **Resistance is not pathologised.** Objections, complaints and whistleblowing
  are not explained as instances of anomie.

### Why "public health"

**Do not blame cholera on the individual's habits. Fix the water supply.** Same structure.

Not "that person is weak" or "that person is arrogant", but **which conditions are
mass-producing that state.** That is what de-individualising means.

---

## 3. Do not stand the order on its head

> Protecting rights makes a society's foundations stronger.

This is true, and it is a **powerful political argument.**
**But it is not the ground for those rights.**

The ground is dignity. The strengthening of the foundations is an argument that
helps institutionalise it — it is a **result**.

**The reason is simple.** Make it the ground, and on the day some data says
*"stripping this group's rights would strengthen the foundations further"*, **the
same logic licenses the violation.**

The same holds for cost. That is why this repository's `03_monetization` is
numbered 03 and not 00.

## 4. Why decision support is undersupplied

This is the structure at the bottom of everything.

**A person deciding for themselves** is an oddly shaped good.

- **The benefit lands on the person.**
- **The burden lands on everyone around them** — family, medicine, welfare, the administration.

What happens when each of them minimises their own burden?

> **The local optimum for every one of them converges on "the person does not decide".**

Nobody has to intend any harm. It happens anyway.
**Nobody is rewarded for supplying it, so nobody supplies it.** The one who should
receive it is the least powerful actor in the room.

Put in the language of a ledger:

> **"Efficiency", in this system, measures how much was pushed onto the person.**

Everyone around lightens their own books, and the cost accrues in the **unrecorded
ledger of the one who cannot refuse** — suppressed intention, load that never
found words, a self that shrank.

## 5. How to count social impact

This is what drives the design.

### Do not make the absence of friction a result

**Zero complaints. Everyone compliant. High retention.**

— these can describe **the most oppressive state there is.**
Saying nothing may mean being satisfied. It may mean having learned that saying
something changes nothing. **Both look identical in the number.**

### So count the opposite

| What is counted | What it signals |
|---|---|
| **Times a record was corrected** | The person could say "that is wrong" |
| **Times something was refused** | There was room to refuse |
| **Times an objection was recorded** | There was somewhere for the objection to stay |
| **Times someone left** | Leaving was available |
| **Times consent was withheld** | Consent was not a formality |

**Friction is not failure. It is evidence that an equilibrium of suppression has broken.**

This is where the refusal to return scores, rankings or verdicts comes from.
Return one, and what is being counted slides back to *the state of the person*.
**What may be counted is what the person was able to do — never what the person is.**

## 6. And the blade points back at us

**The office building this tool is one of the "people around" in §4.**

Fees that track retention structurally prefer users who **do not decide, who stay
where they are put, and who take little looking after.**

So the difference is not good intentions.

> **The difference is recording your own offloading, against your own interest.**

A design that relies on goodwill breaks on the day the goodwill runs out.
So this tool is built to work **the same way when no one happens to be kind.**
Drop that, and this tool becomes the fifth place the cost gets pushed to.

## 7. A drawing from fifteen years ago, and the one thing kept from it

The origin of this idea is **a single diagram drawn about fifteen years ago.**

In it, a person's health information was deposited with a platform as a **commons**,
and the return came back to them as **the right to use their own platform for free.**
It was to be put to work in three directions — research, industry, government —
and the operator was to be held to quality guarantees.

**That form is not being adopted as it stands.** Four reasons.

| | Why |
|---|---|
| **The anonymity premise** | In those years "anonymise it and it is usable" held. Now you design **assuming re-identification** |
| **Chicken and egg** | If the return is "free use of the platform", **there is nothing to return to the first person** |
| **No track record of returns** | Almost no platform of this kind survived domestically. **The arrows can be drawn; evidence of money moving is what is missing** |
| **The word "investment"** | Say "we are inviting investment" where power is unequal, and **the subject slides back to the operator** |

### One thing was kept

> **That the platform reaches people who cannot pay.**

That single point is the question the fifteen-year-old diagram still answers.
What changed is **how it gets there.**

## 8. What we would ask others to carry

This tool holds no model of its own. The listener is an outside model. So what
this tool can reach depends on **whether it reaches that model at all.**

### What we would ask for

**(1) Access itself**

**The resources it takes for a disabled person to get as far as typing a prompt.**
That is the whole request.

**It is not an exchange for data.**
"Free to use, in exchange for letting the diagnosis and traits be used in training"
looks like a deal in which nobody loses. But **the moment a person's record is the
consideration, the record becomes a hostage to the price.** It takes most from
those who have least. So: **no coupling. Access only.**

Whether anything is then shared is a separate decision, and it is the person's.

**(2) "Return no verdicts" — on the model's side too**

This tool holds the line at the input stage: the person's own sentence is read
back verbatim, never rewritten. But **the output stage can only be held by whoever
holds the model.**

- Do not infer and return a person's personality or traits
- Do not produce answers of the form "this person is like this"
- Do not add judgments nobody asked for

**We cannot hold these three completely from our side.**

**(3) Being able to see what happened after it arrived**

Handing out free access and stopping there falls into the same trap as §5.
**Count not how many were handed out, but what people were able to do** — we would
want a hand in designing that.

### What cannot be claimed

This is **a request, not an agreement.**
Nothing has been settled with anyone, and nothing is decided by it being written here.
**It is written as what ought to be.**
